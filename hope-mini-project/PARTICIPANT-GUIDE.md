# Community Agent Practice Lab — Participant Guide

Welcome! Here's what to expect.

## What it is

A free, optional, online practice session focused on working with an AI agent.

## Format

- **Where:** Online
- **Cost:** Free
- **Commitment:** Optional
- **Length:** 30 minutes

## What to bring

An AI agent you already know how to use. No new tool to learn — just come ready to use one you're familiar with.

## What you'll do

- Complete one small project during the session.
- Use only public, synthetic, or non-confidential material — nothing sensitive.
- Your agent will preserve its work in files as it goes.
- Before accepting any result, take a moment to inspect it yourself.

## Wrap-up

The last five minutes are set aside for feedback.

## Good to know

- This session is not recorded.
- No certificate is provided.

That's it — see you there.
